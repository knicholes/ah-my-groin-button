0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer, 20 % infill, PLA or PETG. Four M3 brass heat-set
inserts go into the body's corner blocks; screws are M3 × 25 mm
(foot 8 + tray 3.5 + block 12 = 23.5 mm of stack).

**Check the reported size when you load these.** They are in millimetres.
Until 2026-07-26 the exporter wrote metres, so `body.stl` was 0.2 × 0.13 ×
0.11 — a size a slicer accepts silently. `build_case.py` now reads each STL
back and fails the build if the bounding box is wrong.

## Fit notes for this shape

```
speaker     x[16.10 93.90] y[26.10 103.90]   stack top z=35.00 vs button bottom z=56.50
spk bosses  bolt circle r=41.65   (58.90 mm square pattern)
holder      x[110.53 179.47] y[15.89 64.11] z[3.50 21.30]   PCB underside z=28.50
switch      x[112.83 122.53] y[22.32 33.09]   window x[110.83 124.53] y[20.32 35.09]
end stops   16.91 mm tabs either side of a 20.0 mm lead gap
nub notches r=4.00 on ±Y at y=21.00 / 109.00   ring margin 2.00, wall margin 13.50, flange cover 1.50
```

Two things in there are tighter than they look and would be the first to break
if the box changes shape:

* **Flange cover 1.50 mm.** The button's 99 mm flange hides the notches, whose
  outer radius is 48 mm. There is 1.5 mm of overlap and no more.
* **Battery end stops.** They reach x = 182.275 and the PCB standoff at
  (185, 35) starts at x = 182.0. The 20 mm gap in the end stops is what keeps
  them apart — it is not only there for the leads.
